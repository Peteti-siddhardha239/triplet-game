    ⏳ TIME TROTTER — SIMPLE RULEBOOK

    How To Play & Win

    🎯 1. The Simple Goal (How to Win)

    Your goal is to find 3 matching cards of the exact same number (for example: 🔴 Red 7, 🔵 Blue 7, 🟢 Green 7).

    The first player to get 3 matching sets (Triplets) wins the game!

    🃏 2. The Cards

    Cards are numbered 0 to 9, plus +2 and +4. Every number exists in 3 colors: Red, Blue, and Green. Some cards are in players' hands, and some are face-down on the center board.

    👀 3. Your Turn (You get 2 moves)

    On your turn, you get 2 moves (2 clues) to look at hidden cards:

    • Option A: Ask any player to reveal their Highest or Lowest card.
    • Option B: Click and flip 1 card face-up from the center board.

    ⚡ Cards stay visible for only 4 seconds! Remember where they are!

    🎁 4. Secret Bonus Move!

    If the 2 cards you look at during your turn have the EXACT SAME NUMBER (e.g. you saw two 5s), you instantly get a FREE 3rd Move on that turn!

    🏆 5. Claiming a Triplet (Scoring)

    When you know where all 3 cards of a number are, select the number and click "Claim Triplet"!

    • Right Guess: You lock in that Triplet point!
    • Wrong Guess: You lose your next turn as a penalty!
