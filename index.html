    How to play at the table

    The deck and deal

    The deck has 36 cards: red, blue, and green copies of 0–9, +2, and +4.

    • 3 players: 10 cards each, 2×3 matrix
    • 4 players: 8 cards each, 2×2 matrix
    • 5 players: 6 cards each, 2×3 matrix

    Clues and the free flip

    Each turn gives you 2 clues plus 1 free matrix flip (doesn't cost a clue). Ask an opponent for their high or low card (costs a clue), or flip a matrix card (free once, costs a clue after).

    All reveals vanish after 3–4 seconds. Nothing is written down — memory is the game.

    Bonus clue

    If your first two clues reveal the same card value, you earn a 3rd clue. In Hard mode, both clues must be genuinely new discoveries (not cards you already knew about).

    Memory decay (Hard mode)

    In Hard mode, card locations "fade" after 3 full rounds. You must re-reveal a stale card before you can use it to validate a triplet claim.

    Triplets and victory

    Call a triplet when you know where all three copies of a value are. A wrong call skips your next turn. The first player to complete 3 triplets (Normal) or 4 triplets (Hard) wins. Completing the 7 triplet wins immediately in either mode.

    Turn timer

    In online games: Normal turns last 40 seconds, Hard turns last 25 seconds. The timer resets after each clue action. Time expiry auto-ends your turn.
